FIRE frequency analysis in macrophages

Using the union of FIRE peaks across all time points, differential FIRE regions were identified with Fisher’s exact test for pairwise time-point comparisons during the macrophage LPS time course. Transcription factor motif enrichment was then assessed in the differential FIRE regions.

Differential FIRE frequency

Genome-wide analysis and visualization of changes in FIRE frequency between time points.

Transcription factor motif enrichment

Motif enrichment in differential FIRE regions from pairwise comparisons between 0, 5, 10, and 15 minutes after LPS treatment.

Manhattan distance and Leiden clustering

Fiber-seq reads from the top 10 allele-specific FIRE (AS-FIRE) examples in lymphoblastoid cell lines (LCLs) were clustered using Manhattan distances computed from binary methylation matrices centered on the focal AS-FIRE region, followed by Leiden clustering.

Top 10 LCL AS-FIRE examples

Read-level clustering of binary methylation patterns in 2-kb windows centered on the focal AS-FIRE regions.

Clustering parameter comparison

Comparison of neighbor-count and Leiden resolution settings to assess their effects on clustering at the top-ranked AS-FIRE region.

Autocorrelation

Autocorrelation of single-molecule methylation patterns was analyzed for the top 10 LCL AS-FIRE examples. Autocorrelation profiles were reduced using principal component analysis (PCA), followed by Leiden clustering.

LCL AS-FIRE autocorrelation and clustering

Analysis of methylation-pattern periodicity, clustering of autocorrelation profiles, and allele-specific nucleosome repeat-length estimates.
